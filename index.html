How a swap works here

Same quote path and calldata builders as the official Ammora swap.

  1. Quote — the pool's quoteExactIn is read on-chain through your connected wallet's own provider for every amount you type — never a public RPC.
  2. Build — the SDK builders buildV3NativePoolExactInput / buildV3PoolExactInputForNative produce the router calldata with your slippage and a 20-minute deadline.
  3. Sign — your wallet sends the transaction to the Ammora native router. Selling tAMM first asks for an exact-amount approval, never unlimited.
